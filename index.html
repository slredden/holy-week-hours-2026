Readings from the Revised Common Lectionary, a service of the Vanderbilt Divinity Library · Year A, 2026 · The Hours follow the traditional pattern of Lauds, Terce, Sext, None, Vespers, and Compline. Psalm assignments at each hour are drawn from the day's appointed psalm; all other readings follow the RCL pericopes for Holy Week.
